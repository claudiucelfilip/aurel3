What counts as a correct replication

  1. Freeze the reference: bot build, settings, broker contract, quotes, costs, dates and tester mode.
  2. Record the original trades: run the reference in the historical tester and export orders, deals, settings and logs.
  3. Implement the complete rules: preserve positions, stops and financing across days; use the same market data.
  4. Match every event: direction, time, price, size, exits and costs; investigate every missing or extra trade. Check stop changes wherever observable.
  5. Only then test protection: freeze the matching baseline before adding filters, AI or different sizing.

Replay cases must include overnight/weekend holdings, a new signal while occupied, daylight-saving changes, gaps, and a move followed by a reversal within one minute. Matching one broker’s replay still does not authenticate the seller’s original historical data.
