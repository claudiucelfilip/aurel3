KNOWN DIFFERENCES IN OUR EXISTING EXPERIMENT
1. QQQ ETF and regular-session prices replace the NAS100 broker contract/feed.
2. Every position closes five minutes before the daily close. Multi-day holdings
   and subsequent signals while occupied are impossible in the daily simulator.
3. A custom max(2*ATR14, 0.25% price) stop and completed-minute-close trailing
   were supplied without evidence of vendor equivalence.
4. Risk is 0.5%, with 100% notional cap and synthetic fractional shares, rather
   than the published 1% preset and actual CFD contract/lot/margin rules.
5. EMA input excludes overnight quotes; original EMA history is unspecified.
6. Eligibility requires research-only 200-day-SMA warmup. The four modification
   variants, including AI filtering, are our additions.
