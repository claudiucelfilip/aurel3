Research decision — 21 October 2026
==================================

Verdict: reject the three tested weekly, long/cash specifications for replacing
SPY. This is not a rejection of all multi-asset investing or all machine learning.

All returns below are annualized USD total-return proxies after generic trading
costs, before taxes, FX conversion and subscriptions. Price cutoff: 20 Oct 2026.
Initial capital is an illustrative USD25,000, not the user's verified budget.

                                  2013-latest      2022-latest    Recent drawdown
SPY                                  14.92%            12.04%           -24.50%
Diversified allocation               12.13%             7.65%           -23.50%
90% SPY / 10% Bitcoin                 19.92%            13.20%           -27.91%
Price trends                          7.73%             5.17%            -7.57%
Price + economic trends               7.86%             4.31%            -8.23%
Model-assisted trends                 8.64%             5.36%           -10.76%

The active candidates are much less volatile and spend substantial time in cash.
An ex-post volatility-matched SPY/cash diagnostic earns 7.65%, 7.35% and 7.29%
against price, economic and model strategies, respectively, in 2013-latest.
The model's roughly 1.35 percentage-point advantage on that diagnostic remains
below the desired practical margin. This comparison is diagnostic, not a claim
that scaling the active strategy with leverage would reproduce its return.

38 simulations retained: six main portfolios, execution/cost/skip stresses,
asset-group removals, macro timing and lookback sensitivity. Removing crypto
reduces active annual returns to approximately 2.71%-3.44%. No parameters were
retuned to rescue the failed portfolios.

The passive Bitcoin control benefits from hindsight in asset choice and early
Bitcoin gains. Its recent advantage is only 1.16 percentage points with a worse
drawdown. It is not promoted as a replacement or a predictive algorithm.

Verified
--------
* Frozen strategy specification and SHA256 input/protocol fingerprints.
* Actual first-release CPI/payroll columns; no most-recent values substituted.
* Annual model training excludes unfinished forward labels. Future-price
  perturbation leaves prior model predictions unchanged.
* Cash conservation, fee deductions, no unintended borrowing/shorts, minimum
  tickets, delayed fills and missed-decision paths.
* Zero-cost SPY simulation matches an independently computed buy-and-hold value.
* Ten research tests passed on the downloaded data.

Assumed
-------
* First-release macro data usable after a 90-day lag, stressed to 120 days.
  Original publication timestamps were not supplied by these workbooks.
* ETF cost 25bps each way, crypto 75bps, minimum USD1. These are generic scenarios.
* Fractional adjusted-price units, USD base, 1% NAV trade threshold.
* One cash pool; no broker-to-broker transfer delay or separate-account funding.

Out of scope
------------
* Actual broker availability, tax, FX conversion, integer lots, and subscriptions.
* Futures, options, leveraged FX, margin, financing and liquidation simulation.
* A complete historical instrument universe, live profitability, and a globally
  optimal strategy. The inspected recent period is not an untouched holdout.
* Runtime deployment or changes to live recommendations and portfolio records.

Issues corrected during implementation
-------------------------------------
* Original-release workbooks have a NOTES sheet before their DATA sheet.
* A UTC crypto close labelled Friday is unavailable at the US Friday close.
* Crypto midnight sizing must not use the coming US session's opening prices.
* A five-year learning-history minimum must count completed labels, not older
  rows whose labels are missing. All results were rerun after this correction.

Reproduction
------------
See README.txt. Full outputs, fill ledgers, annual returns, model training logs
and the HTML report live in data/multiasset/results/. The local report's download
bundle includes code, raw inputs and results. Tests validate the research engine;
they do not validate a live investment strategy.

Next evidence needed
--------------------
Test a separately specified long/short global-macro approach using actual
capital-efficient instruments, including financing, margin and manual execution.
Do not infer that leveraged returns can be obtained by multiplying these curves.
Before implementing a live producer, a candidate must clear the return, risk,
execution and broker-feasibility gates. The existing manual desk is the intended
integration point; no producer was activated by this research.
