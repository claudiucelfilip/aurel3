Cash-only and overfitting audit — 21 October 2026
================================================

Decision
--------
No cash-only candidate currently establishes robust outperformance over both
SPY and gold. Stop tuning this family to rescue its return objective. Existing
history can reject it; it cannot supply independent confirmation of a winner.

The user's hard constraint is no borrowing or leveraged approaches. The current
shortlist contains the eight unchanged cap<=1 definitions from the first
redesign: five active rules, SPY, gold, and a monthly 50/50 mix. All 16 cap>1
redesign/allocation rules, the original margined grid and its controls, and
the leveraged annual selector are excluded. Historical runners and results
remain archived for reproduction. No live system or portfolio changed.

Actual ETF history, $10,000, 2019 through 16 October 2026:
  SPY                         $34,702    daily-close drawdown 33.7%
  Gold                        $31,417    daily-close drawdown 26.4%
  Monthly 50/50, cash only     $34,412    daily-close drawdown 20.5%
  Diversified trend, 1x        $27,938    daily-close drawdown 16.1%
Costs: 10bps per traded side, fractional units, no positive-cash interest,
tax, FX or minimum fees. The mix's 2006-start drawdown is 33.0%; cash-only
does not mean low risk. The former 25% screen was our research assumption,
not a maximum loss approved by the user.

Fresh 2022 results: gold $22,578; mix $20,098; relative strength $19,337;
gold trend $19,253; diversified trend $18,006; hold dip $15,180; small-exit
dip $13,909. Every eligible alternative trails gold. The mix beats both at

Adversarial review: cash funding and causality
--------------------------------------------
32 existing cash-only curves contain 80,784 holding rows and 4,945 trade days.
An independent review reproduced every curve from source within $2.8e-9 and
reconstructed a sell-first ledger within $9.5e-11. Minimum interim cash was
about -$4e-11, numerical roundoff. Maximum saved gross exposure was
1.0000000000000004; maximum cumulative funding was below $1e-12. No economic
borrowing or short holdings were found in those records.

The narrower no-future-close-signal-leak claim survived the reviewed tests.
The literal claim that every trade input is known BEFORE execution is REFUTED:
trade quantities use the current opening prices and open NAV, then fill at
those same prices. Example: doubling only the 3 February 2020 gold open in
mix_1x changes the same-open SPY purchase from about $85 to $2,663, despite
identical prior history and SPY open (redesign.py:114-124,159,203).

This is a simultaneous opening-price sizing assumption, not evidence of
future-close leakage. Actual auction orders must be sized beforehand, or
orders submitted after the open require later quotes/fills. Sell-first
funding, settlement, rounding, quote movement and realistic latency remain
unvalidated. Do not label these backtests executable order simulations.
