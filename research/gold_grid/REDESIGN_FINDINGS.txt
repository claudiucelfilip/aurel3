Gold-grid redesign study, 21 October 2026
=======================================

Result: none of the 24 tested rules/controls beat both unleveraged benchmarks
from both 2020 and 2022 while keeping daily-close drawdown at or below 25%.
The direct grid repairs are not justified as a passive-investment replacement.
Simple leveraged diversification beats both at several endpoints, but its
drawdown, funding sensitivity and rolling-period performance remain substantial.

The monthly mix buys $7,500 SPY + $7,500 gold per $10,000 equity, borrowing
$5,000. It resets that allocation monthly. Its annual return since 2006 was
13.8%, versus SPY 11.1% and gold 10.0%. It beat both in only 81/214 overlapping
three-year windows (37.9%). These overlapping windows are not independent.

The volatility-only alternative inverse-vol weights the two assets using
60 previous sessions, scales with their covariance toward 20% annual volatility,
caps target exposure at 1.5x and rebalances weekly. Since 2006 its annual return
was 12.7%, but since 2022 its annual advantage over gold was just 0.63 points.
A volatility target is not a drawdown guarantee.

Verified
--------
- 34 targeted tests pass, including delayed signals, future-price perturbation,
 next-open execution, calendar funding, costs and gaps through stop levels.
- Independent replay: 144 curves, 33,011 trade days, maximum account error
 $0.000000000262. No tested main account became insolvent.
- Both ETF file hashes match the earlier archive manifest. SPY dividends are
 incorporated through adjusted OHLC; GLD fund expenses are embedded.
- A timestamp-resolution mismatch in the initial data alignment was caught
 before interpreting those outputs, fixed and covered by a regression test.
 The displayed outputs were rerun with nanosecond-normalized quote lookup.
- All candidates, neighboring settings, cost stresses and source comparisons
 remain in the downloadable results; no losing candidate is hidden.

Assumed / limits
----------------
- Borrowing 5% annual on actual negative cash; trading 10bps per side. Stress
 uses 10% borrowing and 30bps per side. Positive cash earns zero. No taxes.
- Fractional adjusted-price research units; no verified brokerage loan access,
 margin-call/maintenance policy, minimum fee, FX costs or executable orders.
- Exposure caps apply at rebalance; drift is possible. Maximum close exposure
 for the monthly 1.5x mix was 1.746x; volatility-controlled 1.5x reached 1.569x.
- Extra execution-delay stress moves the order date as well as the signal.
  Stateful dip-ladder latency is excluded; it needs a pending-order model.
- Daily-close drawdown differs from the original grid's minute-path metric.
- First-round SPY daily returns differ from the Yahoo archive by up to 2.69
 percentage points on certain March 2020 dates, although terminal cumulative
 returns closely agree. Cause unresolved. Headline figures use actual ETF
 archive prices; the original reconstruction is preserved separately.
- Every period has been inspected earlier in this research process. Annual
 walk-forward selection is causal but is not a new untouched holdout.

Out of scope
------------
No live Aurel3 policy change, trade, account setup, broker selection, AI return
prediction, tax guidance or assertion of future superiority.
