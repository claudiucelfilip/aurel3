Gold-grid baseline findings — 2026-10-21
========================================

Verified experiment, not exact creator replication.
Source: https://www.tiktok.com/@thequantbuilder/video/7699030814419422497
Data: 2019-01-01 23:00 UTC to 2026-10-19 13:59 UTC, 2,762,399 active minute observations.

Default reconstruction ($10,000 starting account):
- Doubling: $6,022.07 ending equity; -39.78% total return;
  62.74% maximum simulated intraminute drawdown.
- 3,229 winning batches followed by one forced-liquidation loss.
  Profits summed to $6,161.69; the losing batch cost $10,139.62.
- Forced liquidation on 2020-08-12 01:44:00+00:00. Trading stopped permanently then.
  Remaining cash stayed idle through the dataset end. No restart or extra deposit.
- Fixed-size version: $6,778.06; -32.22% total return;
  79.94% worst drawdown; also forced to liquidate.
- Fresh 2022 doubling account: $5,413.30, with a margin exit in July 2022.
- SPY adjusted daily benchmark: $34,183.54; 241.84% total return.
  Its final available close is 2026-10-16; exact gold/stock execution clocks differ.

Sensitivity, not optimization:
- 24 of 27 doubling parameter combinations hit a margin exit; 3 survived.
- All 3 survivors used a 0.1-ounce initial order and $20 spacing. Total returns
  ranged from 31.2% to 85.4%, below SPY over this sample. They are not selected winners.
- 18 of 27 fixed-size combinations hit a margin exit.
- Both assumed intraminute price orderings produced losses and forced exits for
  all four main start-year/sizing baselines, though exact P&L and exit dates differ.
- Higher leverage did not repair the default baseline. Financing assumptions
  materially affect some long-held fixed-size baskets.

Verified:
- 11 targeted tests pass: doubling, fixed sizing, weighted target, costs, funding,
  open losses, forced exits, gaps, rejection, path order, future-data isolation.
- Independent fill-by-fill accounting reconciles 145,333 ledger entries across
  eight main runs with maximum cash error below $0.000000002.
- All mirrored file hashes match the pinned archive tree. Prices exactly match
  194 original-provider daily archives across 2019, 2022, 2023 and 2026 samples.

Assumed:
- The creator's unknown order size, spacing, target, entry rule, capital,
  historical leverage and swaps are NOT recovered. Explicit defaults are in protocol.json.
- M1 high/low paths are synthetic quote paths, not ticks or rigorous result bounds.
- Financing is 5% annual notional, not a historical broker swap schedule.
- Margin exit liquidates the whole basket at 50% of used margin; no partial exits.
- Mirror lacks volume. Entirely flat minutes are omitted; one unmatched quote is excluded.

Out of scope:
- Loss-mitigation variants, AI filters, live deployment and tax treatment.
- No untouched test set is claimed. Every inspected period is now development data.

Interpretation:
The reconstruction displays the advertised failure mechanism: profitable closed
batches can coexist with a large hidden open loss. A very high winning-batch rate
is largely a consequence of only voluntarily closing winners. Adding more gold
when it falls creates concentrated exposure to a prolonged decline. This does
not prove every grid must lose, or that a bounded variant cannot work.

Next: keep this baseline fixed and separately evaluate bounded exposure, limited
additions and basket-loss exits, counting lost recoveries as well as avoided disasters.
